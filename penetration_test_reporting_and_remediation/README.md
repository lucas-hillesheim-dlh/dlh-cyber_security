## Introduction

Strong documentation and reporting skills are essential in Information Security, particularly in penetration testing engagements where clear communication of findings directly impacts remediation and risk reduction.

In this project, we focus on producing a structured and professional penetration test report, including executive summaries, technical findings, attack narratives, and remediation recommendations. The objective is not only to identify vulnerabilities but also to document them in a clear, actionable, and risk-based manner aligned with industry standards.

A penetration test represents a snapshot in time of the target environment. Therefore, the report should clearly define the scope, testing period, methodologies used, and any relevant limitations. This ensures that stakeholders understand the context of the findings and the conditions under which the assessment was performed.

## Learning Objectives
At the end of this project, you are expected to be able to explain to anyone, without the help of Google:

    How to create a professional penetration test report?
    What are the key reporting considerations (audience, timing, format)?
    What are the essential components of a penetration test report?
    How to align documentation specifications and format?
    How to apply different risk scoring methodologies (CVSS, DREAD, OWASP, custom)?
    How to document test limitations and assumptions?
    How to analyze findings and develop actionable recommendations?
    What are technical controls and how to recommend them?
    What are administrative controls and how to recommend them?
    What are operational controls and how to recommend them?
    What are physical controls and how to recommend them?
    How to prioritize remediation efforts across control types?
