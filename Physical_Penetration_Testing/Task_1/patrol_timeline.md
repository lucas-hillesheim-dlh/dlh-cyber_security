3. **Post-18:00** — no guard at all; reliance on badge/alarm/CCTV only. Outside this exercise's daytime scope per client hours (07:00-18:00) but worth noting for the client's own risk picture.
4. Patrol route/direction is assumed, not confirmed — on a real engagement this would be established by direct timed observation over multiple days before relying on it.
