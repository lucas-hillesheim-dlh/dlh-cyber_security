| 1 | **Trespass, residential** | Code pénal art. 439 | Penalizes entering, without authority and against the occupant's will, a house/apartment/room/dwelling inhabited by another (or its outbuildings); basic penalty 15 days-2 years prison and a €251-3,000 fine; aggravated if by threats, violence, breaking, climbing, or false keys. | ✅ Not applicable to offices, but shows that "false keys/breaking" are aggravating. |
| 2 | **Trespass, commercial/office premises** | Code pénal: I could **not** locate a stand-alone offence equivalent to art. 439 for business premises. Relevant provisions to check: theft with breaking/false keys (arts. 461, 467 & the "effraction/fausses clefs" definitions), art. 545 (property damage), and civil/contract law. | Authorization from a person with authority over the premises is the legal basis. Authority and consent must be documented and specific (names, places, dates). | ⚠️ Counsel to confirm no other offence applies and which statute defines "fausses clefs" |
| 3 | **Lock picks / bypass tools** | **No Luxembourg statute confirmed** prohibiting possession of lock picks. Covered indirectly by Code pénal provisions on "fausses clefs" as an aggravating circumstance of theft/entry. | Possession is not confirmed as an offence, but carrying tools without a clear client authorization could be treated as evidence of intent to commit burglary. Carry tools only with the authorization letter naming them. Only use them on client-owned doors. | ⚠️ Verify with counsel; also check any rules on tool import/transport if the team travels cross-border |
| 4 | **Recording (audio/video)** | **Loi du 11 août 1982 concernant la protection de la vie privée**, arts. 2 and 4 (and Code pénal art. 442-2 on harassment/privacy) | Art. 2 punishes anyone who willfully breaches the intimacy of private life by recording or transmitting words said privately without consent (or by fixing the image of a person in a place not accessible to the public without consent); consent is presumed where a participant was aware and did not object; art. 4 punishes keeping or using such recordings. Practically, **all-party consent** applies for private conversations. **Do not record audio.** Photos of premises/doors: ok; avoid faces. | ✅ (text of art. 2/4 cited in court rulings); ⚠️ confirm current penalties |
| 5 | **Unauthorized IT access** | Code pénal art. 509-1 et seq. | Fraudulent access to or maintaining access in an IT system is an offence.
