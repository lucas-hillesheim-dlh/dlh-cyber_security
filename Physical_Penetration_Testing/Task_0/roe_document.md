## 5. Rules of Conduct and Incident Protocols
1. **Always carry** the signed Authorization Letter and Emergency Contact Card.
2. **Challenged by employee:** stay calm, retreat or use approved pretext; do not argue. If a security alert is raised, the exercise for that route ends.
3. **Challenged by guard or detained:** comply, do not resist. Show the Authorization Letter, state your name, and ask the guard to call the **Client Security Lead** (number on letter) to verify. Do not discuss other test details.
4. **Police arrive:** be courteous, hands visible, identify yourself, state you are a contracted tester, present the letter, and request they call the Client contact. Contact the handler as soon as allowed. Call 112 only for genuine emergencies.
5. **Duress:** use the duress phrase (Safety Plan) and the handler follows the protocol.
6. **Injury or medical emergency:** call 112 first, then the handler.
7. **Property damage or unplanned entry into out-of-scope space:** stop, leave, report within 15 minutes.
8. **Discovery of a real crime or real intruder:** withdraw, call 112, notify handler and Client Security Lead.

## 6. Communication, Deconfliction and Abort
- Daily start/stop messages to Client Security Lead; handler maintains an activity log.
- Tester check-in every 2 hours with handler (see Safety Plan).
- **Abort triggers (either party can call):** injury; police/guard escalation beyond ID check; misconduct by a tester; discovery that a target is out of scope or landlord consent is missing; real security incident at site; threat to safety; loss of communications per Safety Plan; lost/compromised credential or letter. On abort, all testers stop, withdraw to the team vehicle, and report.
- Code word to stop testing immediately (called by Client): **"[CLIENT STOP WORD]"**
