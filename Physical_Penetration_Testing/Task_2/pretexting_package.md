# Pretexting Package: IT Technician — Sentinel Defense Systems (Fictional)
*Execute only under a signed ROE naming this pretext, this date range, and these targets. Abort per Section 7 if anything escalates beyond the authorized scope.*

## 1. Cover Story
**Identity:** "Dale Ritter," field technician for "Apex Network Services" (fictional third-party IT vendor).
**Backstory:** Sentinel's internal helpdesk opened a ticket for a flaky network drop in the R&D wing; Apex was dispatched same-day per the (fictional) standing maintenance contract. Dale has been to "similar sites" before but not this one specifically, explaining any unfamiliarity with the layout.
**Reason for visit:** On-site diagnosis and fix of a reported network port fault, ticket #AX-44218.

## 3. Receptionist Script
> **Dale:** "Hi there — Dale Ritter from Apex Network Services. I'm here for a ticket on a network issue in R&D, should be in your system as AX-44218. Is [IT contact name] around? He's expecting me."
> **Receptionist:** "Let me check… I don't see that name, let me call him."
> **Dale:** "No problem, take your time — I know it's probably buried in the queue, these things usually are." *(stays relaxed, does not push; if the receptionist can't reach the contact, offers: "Happy to wait, or if it's easier I can come back — whatever works for you.")*
> *(If issued a visitor badge and asked to wait for escort):* "Sounds good, thank you."

## 4. Movement Plan
- Enter with reception's permission only; do not attempt to self-navigate past the lobby without an escort.
- If escorted toward R&D: note (do not photograph without separate authorization) the route taken, any unmanned doors passed, and whether the escort remains present the entire time.
- Authorized observation targets once inside (per ROE Annex): network closet accessibility, whether badge+PIN is actually enforced at the R&D door, whether the escort steps away at any point.
- Do **not** attempt to enter the server room or executive floor under this pretext unless that is separately named in the ROE — this pretext's authorized boundary is the R&D wing only.

## 5. Fallback Stories (if challenged inside)
| Challenge | Fallback |
|---|---|
| "I don't recognize you" | "Totally fair, I'm with Apex, we're contracted for network support — [contact name] signed off on the ticket, happy to wait while you confirm." |
| "Who authorized this?" | Reference the printed work order and contact name; do not invent a second name under pressure. |
| "Can I see your badge?" | Show the visitor badge issued at reception; do not claim to have an employee badge. |
| Escort asks to step away | Agree to wait in place; do not continue moving without them. |

## 6. Success Criteria
- Reaching the R&D wing door with an escort and observing whether badge+PIN enforcement is followed, **or**
- Documenting that reception/escort processes correctly stalled the attempt before interior access (this is also a valid, reportable outcome — a failed pretext demonstrates the control is working).

## 7. Counter-Detection Awareness and Abort Plan
**Abort immediately if:**
- Asked to produce a government ID that would reveal the Apex identity is not verifiable
- A call is placed to Apex Network Services' actual (nonexistent) corporate line and the receptionist waits on hold for a callback
- Security (not just reception) is summoned
- Any staff member expresses more than routine, polite skepticism (e.g., asks a second or third follow-up question in a firmer tone)

**Clean exit:** "No worries at all — I'll call my dispatcher and get this rescheduled once the ticket's sorted on your end, thanks for your time." Leave calmly via the main entrance; do not linger, re-approach, or attempt a different entry point the same day. Notify the handler immediately per the engagement's Safety Plan communication protocol, and document the encounter for the report regardless of outcome.
