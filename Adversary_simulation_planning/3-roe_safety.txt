Rules of Engagement Addendum & De-confliction Protocol
Exercise: FIN7 Threat Emulation

Part A - Safe Simulation Table:

| Behavior                   | Risk Level | What Is Allowed                                                                                      | What Is Prohibited                                                                                      | Cleanup Steps                                                                                          |
|-----------------------------|------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| C2 Beacon                   | Medium     | Simulated outbound beacon traffic to a pre-approved, operator-controlled test listener on an isolated lab network, at a fixed low interval. | Beaconing to any live/public internet infrastructure, real malware C2 frameworks pointed at production assets, or traffic that leaves the lab network segment. | Terminate the beacon process; remove any scheduled task or script used to start it; confirm no outbound connections remain via netstat/ss; clear related temp files. |
| Persistence (Registry Run Key) | Low        | Adding a single benign test entry (pointing to a harmless, non-executing or clearly labeled test binary) to a Run key on an isolated lab VM. | Modifying production host registries, adding entries that execute real payloads, or persisting across a VM snapshot revert. | Delete the specific Run key value immediately after the test step; verify removal with a registry query; revert VM to clean snapshot if available. |
| Data Exfiltration           | High       | Simulated transfer of clearly labeled, synthetic dummy data (no real PII/PCI/financial records) to a lab-internal destination over an approved protocol. | Exfiltrating any real client data, real credentials, or any data leaving the authorized lab network boundary; use of real cloud storage or external drop sites. | Delete the dummy dataset and any staged copies from source and destination; verify no residual files in temp/staging directories; confirm transfer logs are retained for the report, not the data itself. |
| Lateral Movement            | High       | Simulated authentication/movement between pre-designated lab hosts using test or disposable credentials created solely for the exercise. | Using real employee or service-account credentials, moving to any host outside the agreed lab scope, or movement into production segments. | Remove any test credentials, scheduled tasks, or remote sessions created; reset lab host states to baseline; confirm no new accounts or sessions persist. |
| Credential Access            | Medium     | Simulated credential-dumping technique run against a lab VM seeded only with dummy/throwaway credentials. | Dumping or capturing real user, admin, or service-account credentials on any system, in-lab or otherwise. | Securely delete any dumped credential artifacts and output files; rotate/delete the dummy accounts used; confirm no credential material is left in memory dumps, logs, or temp storage. |

Part B - De-confliction Protocol:

1. Point of Contact:
   If the Blue Team detects suspicious activity, they contact the designated
   Exercise Lead (Red Team Lead) directly via the pre-agreed out-of-band
   channel (dedicated Slack/Teams de-confliction channel or phone bridge
   established before the exercise begins). The Exercise Lead is reachable
   for the full duration of the test window.

2. Validation Process:
   The Exercise Lead cross-checks the reported activity (timestamp, source
   host/IP, behavior) against the signed-off Emulation Plan and live
   operator log. Validation is targeted to complete within 15 minutes of
   the Blue Team's report. If the Exercise Lead cannot reach the operator
   or confirm the activity within that window, it is treated as unvalidated
   and escalated per Section 4.

3. Test Continuation:
   The specific test step under question is paused the moment the Blue
   Team reports it, while the rest of the exercise may continue only if
   the paused step is isolated and does not affect other in-progress
   steps. Once the Exercise Lead confirms the activity is part of the
   authorized simulation, the paused step resumes. If it cannot be
   confirmed within the validation window, the entire exercise pauses.

4. Real Incident Discovery:
   If the activity is confirmed NOT to be part of the simulation, the
   exercise is immediately and fully paused. The Exercise Lead notifies
   the client's Incident Response lead and CISO (or designated security
   point of contact) without delay, hands off all relevant timing and
   scope details to the client's incident response process, and the
   simulation does not resume until the client confirms the real incident
   has been triaged and gives explicit authorization to continue.

Part C - Emergency Stop Criteria:

1. Unintended Scope Breach: Any simulated activity (beaconing, lateral
   movement, credential access, etc.) is detected on a system, network
   segment, or account outside the explicitly agreed lab/test boundary.

2. Real Alert Triggered in a Production System: A genuine security alert,
   SIEM correlation, or incident ticket is generated against production
   infrastructure that is not accounted for by the signed-off Emulation
   Plan.

3. System Instability or Service Disruption: Any simulation step causes a
   crash, performance degradation, service outage, or otherwise impacts
   the availability of a lab or production system beyond what was
   explicitly planned and approved.

4. Loss of Communication with the Exercise Lead or Operator: The
   de-confliction point of contact becomes unreachable during an active
   test step, removing the ability to validate or control the simulation
   in real time.

5. Client-Issued Stop Order: The client, at any time and for any reason,
   invokes the agreed stop-word or formally requests the exercise be
   halted; this is honored immediately and without requiring
   justification.
