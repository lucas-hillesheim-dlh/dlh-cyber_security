## What is Cyber Forensic Investigation

Forensic cyber security is the application of forensic science to the investigation of cybercrimes. It is used to gather and analyze digital evidence from computers, networks, and other electronic devices to help solve cybercrimes and bring criminals to justice. Forensic cyber security experts use a variety of scientific techniques, including data recovery, malware analysis, and intrusion detection, to gather and analyze evidence. They also work to reconstruct cybercrime scenes and to identify victims.

## Why does the company need cyber forensics investigators

Cybercrime is a major global issue that has been in the news a lot lately. It poses a threat to individuals and organizations of all sizes, but especially large international companies, banks, and governments. In the past, cybercrime was often solved by traditional forensic techniques, such as fingerprint analysis and DNA testing. However, as cybercrimes have become more sophisticated, traditional forensic techniques have become less effective. Forensic cyber security experts are needed to use new forensic techniques to investigate cybercrimes and bring criminals to justice.

Digital forensics is broad with many sub-disciplines. It includes computer forensics, mobile forensics, network forensics, and web forensics.

Digital forensics is the process of recovering, analyzing, and preserving digital evidence. It can be used to solve crimes, investigate misconduct, and protect company property. Digital forensics experts use a variety of tools and techniques to gather evidence, including data recovery, malware analysis, and intrusion detection.

## How is Digital Forensics Process

The steps involved in digital forensics can vary depending on the context, but it typically consists of four core steps: collection, examination, analysis, and reporting.“

**Collection**: The collection stage encompasses obtaining digital proof, often through the seizure of physical assets like computers, hard drives, or mobile devices. Ensuring data integrity and avoiding loss during acquisition is crucial. This can be achieved by copying storage media or generating duplicates of the original content.

**Examination**: The examination phase revolves around recognizing and extracting data. This stage can be broken down into preparatory, extraction, and identification steps. Before data extraction, a decision must be made regarding whether to operate on a live or inactive system. For example, a laptop can be powered on for a live examination, or a hard drive can be connected to a laboratory computer.

**Analysis**: The analysis phase involves using collected data to prove or disprove a case built by the examiners. Here are key questions examiners need to answer for all relevant data items:

- Who created the data
- Who edited the data
- How the data was created
- When these activities occur

Moreover, examiners establish the connections between the information and the case’s context.

**Reporting**: The reporting phase involves synthesizing the data and analysis into a format that makes sense to laypeople. These reports are essential because they help convey the information so that all stakeholders can understand.

## Basic tools of Digital Forensic

Here are some of the most popular digital forensics tools:

- **FTK Imager**: A data preview and imaging tool used to acquire data (evidence) in a forensically sound manner by creating copies of data without making changes to the original evidence.
- **EnCase**: A suite of digital investigations products that can be used to recover evidence from seized hard drives, conduct in-depth analysis of user files, and collect evidence such as documents, pictures, internet history, and Windows Registry information.
- **ProDiscover**: A suite of computer forensics and incident response tools that includes diagnostic and evidence collection tools for corporate policy compliance investigations and electronic discovery.
- **Autopsy**: A free and open-source digital forensics platform that makes it easier to deploy many of the open source programs and plugins used in The Sleuth Kit.
- **Wireshark**: The world’s leading network traffic analyzer that can be used to analyze network traffic in real time.
- **Sysinternals**: A website that offers technical resources and utilities to manage, diagnose, troubleshoot, and monitor a Microsoft Windows environment.
- **MOBILedit Forensic Express**: A phone and cloud extractor, data analyzer, and report generator that can be used to extract data from mobile phones and cloud storage.
- **Browser History Examiner**: A forensic software tool for capturing, analyzing, and reporting internet history from the main desktop web browsers.
- **Forensic Express**: A digital forensics tool that offers maximum functionality at a fraction of the price of other tools.

These are just a few of the many digital forensics tools that are available. The specific tools that you need will depend on the type of investigation you are conducting and the type of evidence you are looking for.
